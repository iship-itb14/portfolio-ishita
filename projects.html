Stationery Market Analytics

Amazon review mining and an interactive dashboard, built on 26,700+ listings and 60,000 reviews.

Details

Classified 18 product types from titles. Price only weakly predicts rating (Spearman rho 0.10), and doubling pack size cuts price per item by 37 to 52%. TF-IDF logistic regression and aspect extraction surfaced top complaints by product type, and a gradient boosting model (5-fold CV AUC 0.77) flags listings rated under 4.2 stars. Shown in a five-page dashboard built with Python, scikit-learn and Chart.js.

Antarctic Penguin Population Mining

Spatio-temporal mining of 5,400+ MAPPPD nest-count records across 6 species and 9 CCAMLR regions, 1892 to 2025.

Details

Engineered colony-level trend labels from regression slopes and trained a class-balanced Random Forest (GridSearchCV) that predicts growing, stable or declining colonies at 81% accuracy. K-Means clustering (silhouette 0.83) separated Ross Sea mega-colonies from Peninsula colonies and surfaced a post-2000 Adelie decline. Findings are presented on a public project website.
